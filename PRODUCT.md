# Product

## Register
product

## Users & Purpose
- **Users**: Oliver Arensbach operating Billede Print Studio (photo printing service for portrait prints, school/communion events, contact groups, and private orders).
- **Purpose**: Fast, reliable print studio operations: order logging, toner lifecycle tracking, price calculation, payment tracking, and profit accounting across mobile and PC.
- **Workflow**: Logging print orders in seconds, monitoring real paper/toner wear cost vs revenue, managing printer toner cartridges, and tracking debt/payments.

## Brand & Personality
- **Tone**: Nordic Precision & Clarity — clean, quiet, legible, slate/indigo aesthetic, zero text clutter.
- **Principles**:
  1. *Frictionless entry*: Adding an order or splitting across toners should take 5 seconds.
  2. *Graphic over verbose*: Status badges, clean numbers, and intuitive controls rather than instructional text.
  3. *Honest data*: Actual real-world print counts without artificial caps or misleading progress bars.

## Anti-References
- No generic SaaS cards with decorative left-border stripes or gradient text.
- No patronizing micro-text or tooltip spam.
- No fake capacities or inaccurate countdown progress bars.
